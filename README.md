# Meme Coin Radar

Step 1 foundation • specification v0.1 • 2026-10-01

A separate research project for an eventual 24/7 Discovery Radar that identifies unusually strong meme-coin candidates using evidence available at the time. This repository has no connection to HVAC Opportunity Radar.

## Current engineering milestone

This workspace now includes the initial immutable candidate and observation foundation for Discovery Radar. The implementation preserves timestamped evidence for token identity, market context, structure/safety, narrative analysis (including explicit `NO_NARRATIVE_FOUND`), social research, smart-wallet evidence, attention, project credibility, catalysts, FOMO verification and outcome tracking. It is intentionally a schema + validation foundation only; no paid APIs, trading, subscriptions, or automatic execution are connected.

The schema uses chain + contract identity, explicit provenance, UTC timestamps and append-only/as-of reconstruction for historical evidence. FOMO executability is recorded as `TRUE`, `FALSE` or `UNVERIFIED` and never forced into a positive state.

**Find → Alert → human entry decision → Exit Intelligence.**

The actionable universe is limited to coins with verified, fresh FOMO eligibility and tradability. WATCH means a candidate merits observation. HIGH means the strongest evidence under a prospectively validated policy; it is not a buy command, position-size recommendation, or promise of returns. Zero or multiple HIGH alerts are acceptable. There is no daily quota and no lowering the quality bar during quiet periods.
